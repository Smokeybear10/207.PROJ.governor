## Award tracks

Each team can win only one track but can submit to all that apply.

- **Save Time. Save Money.** — anything that helps people save time, save money, or work smarter. Prize: Switch 2s
- **Best Use of Our Sponsors** — Cursor and Codex credits
- **Best Game** — Oura Rings
- **Audience Favorite** — crowd votes at the demo stations. Prize: ModRetros

## Sponsor tracks

### Codex — Best Use of OpenAI API/Codex

Build something with Codex and/or the OpenAI API to solve a challenging
problem, create a new product, or a creative hack.

Prizes: 1st $10,000 / 2nd $5,000 / 3rd $2,500 in OpenAI API credits.
Top 3 get forwarded to OpenAI. Winning team also gets 6 months of ChatGPT Pro.

Codex credits: redemption email arrives after check-in and the opening
ceremony (check spam). Tyler (Ramp intern, Codex Community Ambassador) is the
on-site contact.

### Cursor — Best Community Impact Hack

Pick a community you care about and turn an idea for it into something real.
Prize: 6 months of Cursor Pro per team member, or equivalent credits.
